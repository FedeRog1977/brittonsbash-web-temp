# Projects

This blog article is entitles "Projects", however it's worth mentioning my other sports before
proceeding. It's also worth mentioning that I look for three things in sport: fitness, calorie burn,
and experience. While all of the sports I do are fine arts, they mostly do all simply supplement the
only one which ticks all three of those boxes the most: "Projects". Given the perfect day for a
"Roadie" though, they can also fall into this highest tier.

## Miles

- Fitness
- Calorie burn

"Miles" began simply as walking laps of my local area. Most commonly, the famous Waterfoot
figure-of-eight. It also extended to some longer laps of areas such as Thorntonhall. More recently
however, "Miles" has extended to the sport of running.

Throughout summer, I would always rely on cycling as my primary fitness sport, then Waterfoot laps
in winter. But it became increasingly unappetising to set off on a ten-mile walk in the freezing
cold rain in the winter months, for little to no reward fitness wise. The Waterfoot laps then
adopted a run leg, which involved walking up to Waterfoot, then running back; splitting the route
50/50 walk/run.

As my running fitness improved, and as I'm sure you can imagine as it got more and more boring to
wait for the run leg, I ditched the walk-in section and started simply running the north-most loop
of the original Waterfoot figure-of-eight.

This resulted in the perfect delivery of what I sought in the "Miles" activity type: maintaining a
high fitness level throughout the weekdays (non-"Project"-days), without dedicating too much time;
in a sport which is also doable all year round, in whatever weather and at whatever time of the day.

## Projects

- Fitness
- Calorie burn
- Experience

"Projects" gained my love for so many reasons. Biggest of all being that it is the no-bullshit
sport. What does that mean? Well, there's no fucking about. It's you, your will, and your fitness
embracing something that will beat you every single time you challenge it. With each repetition,
it's just a game of how well can you live up to the requirements of nature. It's also the one sport
which, while I love the gear, you don't need any stupid shit for. A good pair of B1 boots, the right
layering system, and you're on your way. No posing. No bullshit. It doesn't matter if your gear is
your dad's from the 80's, treat leather and down right, and it will last multiple lifetimes.

Oh and by the way, for some context, "Projects" refer to hiking. Although recently have seen the
addition of track cycle-ins under their umbrella too.

## Tennis

- Experience

Tennis sort of just hovers in the sidelines, hence no nickname. As far as the sport itself goes, not
playing it, it remains one of my biggest passions. Or at least it was until Roger retired. I used to
play it a hell of a lot more in the 2000's and 2010's when Roger was in his prime, and I'd be
inspired daily when watching his matches or highlight compilations. Now though, it takes quite a bit
of effort to get me on the court. My passion mostly just extends to collecting classic tennis gear
now, to be honest.
